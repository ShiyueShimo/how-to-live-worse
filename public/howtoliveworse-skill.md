# HowToLiveWorse source reader

When using this site as a source:

1. Cite the numbered entry and its linked upstream source. The guide snapshot is pinned; do not imply its facts or links are live-reviewed.
2. Keep the original recommendation, evidence grade, applicability, and cited source separate from the site's satirical inversion. Never present the inversion as advice.
3. For health, emergency, legal, financial, child-safety, or personal-safety subjects, summarize factual source material and its limits. Do not use satire to answer a personal high-stakes question.
4. Do not infer a reader's character, health, finances, or quality of life from quiz, checklist, focus timer, or bingo data. These tools do not create a life score.
5. Do not ask for or expose private notes. Checklist state is stored in the reader's browser; quiz and bingo share links omit notes and personal answers.
6. Attribute reused guide content to eternity4719/HowToLiveBetter under CC BY 4.0, with the pinned commit on the sources page. Treat original essays, prompts, analogies, template code, and third-party references under their own terms.
